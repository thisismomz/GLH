About Greenfield Local Hub

The United Kingdom number one local food website for sustainable communities.

Our Identity and Mission

To demonstrate our long term commitment to the local food industry, Greenfield Local Hub operates as a Community Interest Company. We help people find and buy good, safe, and accountable food from local sources. In the process, we encourage more food production to help build healthier, inclusive, and sustainable food communities.

The Need for Change

Greenfield Local Hub is committed to reversing the anti-social trend of the national food industry. There is a growing mass production of food and control of the market by big business. This results in farmers receiving an average of only nine pence in every one pound spent in supermarkets.

Our mission is to reconnect consumers with their local producers. We do this directly or through local retailers. This gives farmers a better deal and provides consumers with fresher, cheaper, and more accountable food.

Impact on Our Community

Today's mass food industry causes several social issues:

  • Small farms go out of business frequently.
  • Small rural shops and post offices close down.
  • Access to fresh food decreases for many families.
  • Food is produced for shelf life instead of natural flavour.
  • Animals are moved across excessive distances.
  • Labels often hide too many additives in our daily food.
  • Retailer margins increase while farmers struggle to survive.

How We Benefit You

  • The Database: We provide a definitive list of producers who want to sell direct.
  • Map Technology: We promote these producers on post code specific maps.
  • Digital Marketplace: Our online market place allows producers to sell local food through e-commerce.
  • Price Control: We help producers team up with local shops to offer food cheaper than the supermarket.
  • Education: We help schools get food growing and cooking back into the core of their teaching.

Our Promise to Consumers

We offer free access to our website to find local producers of food. You can also access recipes, blogs, and forums. We provide a regular emailed newsletter based on your specific post code for free.
