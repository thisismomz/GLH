Welcome to our marketplace! We understand that privacy and security are important. To ensure a safe, legal, and professional environment for both you and your customers, we require certain information during onboarding.
Below are the detailed reasons why each piece of data is essential. 1. Identity & Legal Verification
Full Legal Name & Date of Birth:
Why: We are legally required to verify the identity of everyone selling on our platform to prevent fraud and comply with "Know Your Customer" (KYC) and Anti-Money Laundering regulations.
National Insurance (NI) or Company Registration Number:
Why: As a digital platform, we must report the income of our sellers to HMRC annually. These identifiers ensure that your tax records are accurately linked to your business activity.
2. Food Safety & Compliance
Local Authority Food Business Registration:
Why: It is a legal requirement in the UK for anyone selling food regularly to be registered as a food business with their local council at least 28 days before trading.
Digital Traceability Records:
Why: New 2026 Traceability Regulations mandate that all food products be tracked digitally "from farm to fork." This allows for rapid recalls (within 24 hours) if a food safety issue ever arises.
Allergen & Labelling Information:
Why: By law, consumers must have access to the same level of allergen and ingredient information online as they would find on a physical product label.
3. Financial & Payment Security
Bank Account Details:
Why: We need this information to set up secure automated payouts so you receive your funds directly and on time.
Tax Compliance Status:
Why: UK rules for digital platforms (effective from 2024/2025) require us to verify that sellers are aware of their tax obligations, especially if they exceed the £1,000 trading allowance.
4. Professional Storefront & Trust
Physical Address & Farm Name:
Why: Transparency builds trust. Customers are more likely to buy from a farm they can identify locally. Legally, your business's legal name and address must be accessible on the website.
Product Photos & Descriptions:
Why: High-quality visuals are your best marketing tool. Since 2026 regulations also restrict the advertising of "less healthy" foods online, clear and accurate product descriptions help us ensure your listings remain compliant.
5. Data Privacy (GDPR)
How we use your data:
All information provided is stored securely and handled in strict accordance with UK GDPR. We only collect what is strictly necessary to fulfill our legal obligations and provide you with a functional selling platform.
