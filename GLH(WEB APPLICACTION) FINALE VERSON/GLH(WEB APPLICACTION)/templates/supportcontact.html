Common Questions

How do I know where my food comes from?

Every product on GLH has full traceability. You can click on any item to see the specific farm or maker profile.

What are the benefits of buying local?

Buying through GLH reduces food miles, supports the rural economy, and ensures farmers get a fair price for their work.
